<h2 id="about">About</h2>

I'm the Founder · CTO · Managing Director of **SAYANJALI NEXUS PRIVATE LIMITED**, a technology company based in Hyderabad, India. I design and build software across artificial intelligence, blockchain infrastructure, enterprise automation, cybersecurity, and developer tooling.

The work is organized and presented publicly through **SYJ LABS**, the independent technology lab operated under SAYANJALI NEXUS PRIVATE LIMITED.

**Mission** — Build software that removes friction from real operations: compliance, communication, security, and data — without unnecessary complexity.

**Vision** — Build an ecosystem where AI, blockchain and automation infrastructure are open, auditable and useful to the businesses and developers that adopt them.

<h2 id="what-i-build">What I Build</h2>

**Core Domains**
- Artificial Intelligence & applied LLM systems
- Enterprise automation & workflow platforms
- Blockchain infrastructure & tokenomics
- Cybersecurity, OSINT & threat tooling
- Developer tools & CLI platforms

**Working Principles**
- Local-first AI over unnecessary hosted dependencies
- Complete, documented deliverables rather than partial snippets
- Resource-conscious engineering across supported platforms
- Verification through tests and end-to-end checks where documented
- Honest scope — no overstated claims
